    Mars Atlas

    An interactive map of the Martian surface built on NASA open planetary data. Drag to rotate the globe, scroll to zoom, right-drag to tilt. Every named feature comes from the IAU Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature; elevations are sampled live from the MOLA/HRSC blended digital elevation model.

    • 3D / 2D — switch between a globe and an equirectangular map.
    • Basemaps — natural-colour Viking mosaic or colourised topography.
    • Measure — click points for geodesic distances.
    • Terrain — real elevation, exaggeration up to 3×.
    • Lighting — cinematic atmosphere, solar flare and optional solar wind. These are procedural visuals, not observatory data.

    Imagery and elevation © NASA / JPL-Caltech / USGS / ESA. Nomenclature © IAU WGCCRE. Data served live from NASA Mars Trek. Built with CesiumJS. Solar effects are visualisations aligned to Cesium’s Sun direction, not measured solar imagery.
